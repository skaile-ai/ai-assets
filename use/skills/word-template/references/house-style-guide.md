# House Style Guide

Extracted from the bundled standard document template.

## Layout & Template
- The space below the logo is protected and must remain free.
- Headline numbering is preset in the template; undo it via the numbering menu if not wanted.
- Heading hierarchy: no deeper than three levels if possible.
- Footer (from page 2): title of document, date, person responsible; pages are numbered.
- Bullet points: green and square (available in the bullet point library).

## Punctuation & Typography
- "Quotation marks" are straight, not typographic.
- Internet and SharePoint references are formatted as links (e.g. www.example.com).
- Foreign-language words are in italics (e.g. *in vitro* studies).
- No double spaces; no manual line breaks for alignment (they ruin later reformatting).
- Headings have no full stop.

## Numbers, Units & Currency
- Numbers one to twelve in plain text are written out; 13 and up as numerals. Numbers in tables are always numerals.
- Non-breaking space between number and unit.
- Currency up to a million: ISO three-letter code + non-breaking space + amount — EUR 100. In plain text, omit decimal places if zero.
- Millions and above abbreviated with capital M, B, etc. — USD 150M.
- CHF only, for exclusively Swiss audiences: apostrophe thousands separator and decimal point — CHF 1'500'000.00.

## Dates
- Plain text: English format — 25 August 2026.
- Tables and regulatory/scientific documents: ISO 8601.

## Abbreviations & Trademarks
- Write the full name on first mention with the abbreviation in brackets — Orphan Drug Designation (ODD) — then use only the abbreviation.
- Trademark/registration symbols only on first mention — ValidaTe®, simply ValidaTe thereafter.

## Lists
- Use full stops after every bullet point or after none at all.
- Keep bullets grammatically consistent (all with verbs, or all as nouns).
